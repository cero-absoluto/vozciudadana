# Participation Verification Methodology

Voice Protest — v1.5 candidate,31 October2026. This describes the IDV-08–12
candidate, not a production certification. Publication evidence will identify the
final source and deployed version. Version1.4 is preserved as a historical reference.

## What a participation record means

A record is support associated with a verified identifier and a verification
method for one event. Identifier-specific nullifiers and database constraints
control duplicates. They do not establish one independent human per record,
civil identity, exclusive control, or independence between several accounts.
Telephone and email identifiers are not globally merged into one person.

## Admission and geography

National phone admission uses the versioned numbering-country resolution policy
R-N2, not SIM-registration country, nationality, residence, or physical location.
Shared or unresolved numbering ranges are not silently assigned a country.
Regional and global admission retain their existing openness: regional scope
alone does not impose the national numbering-country restriction.

Institutional-domain events require the email method, including without a census.
The creator declares the domain and institution. Syntax validation and an email
OTP do not independently verify their official relationship, endorsement, role,
student status, employment, or current institutional affiliation.

High/critical-risk and institutional flows exclude GPS from the supported client,
processing and persistence routes. A modified client can still transmit arbitrary
HTTP coordinates; exclusion from processing does not prevent their receipt.
Where allowed, GPS may be supplied initially or reinforced later with a single-use
token before closure. A geocoder failure cannot confirm GPS and does not consume
the reinforcement token. Browser coordinates can be spoofed or stale.

Territorial agreement means declared GPS with matching geocoded country and
municipality/region name. Legacy OSM numbers lack object type and cannot prove
entity identity. This is not polygon containment or certified presence; names,
languages and same-country homonyms remain limitations. No country is inferred
from the first letters of a localized name.

## Census and founder bootstrap

The census is an account-based peer-vouching network, not an official register.
The founder path is tied to the existing creation-key proof and email adhesion.
Its approved first-wave exception allows direct founder accreditation. Subsequent
waves require two endorsements from distinct accredited accounts. Distinct
accounts do not establish distinct or independent people. This version changes
none of those admission or founder rules. The visual milestone30 is not an
institution-size denominator. Participation rate remains absent unless an
explicit registered and auditable eligible universe is available; the current
report route does not invent or supply one.

## Credential age, authentication and eligibility

An SMS/email OTP proves control of that channel at its verification time. A
reusable device credential proves possession of that credential at authentication;
it does not imply a fresh OTP for each adhesion or current control of a recycled
phone number. The documented270-day inactivity rule concerns device-record retention, not
freshness of identity proof or institutional eligibility.

Institutional membership records are assigned the next31August00:00UTC boundary. It is a product expiry
rule, not proof that affiliation continues until then. Retention, authentication
and eligibility are distinct. Fresh OTP verification can refresh channel-control
evidence; there is no claim of automatic periodic renewal, external role checks,
or universal revocation after a change of phone owner/institutional status.
Those lifecycle controls require separate policy/implementation decisions.

## Reports, histories and uncertainty

The report reads recorded verification_method values. Known recorded methods are
phone_otp,institutional_email_otp,qr,eid. Missing or unrecognized historical values
remain unknown; configuration is not evidence that those rows used email or SMS.
A recorded method is not a claim that an excluded historical provider proof was
independently revalidated. IP/GPS are described as recorded signals; histories
are not recalculated or upgraded to newly obtained corroboration.

The corrected admission index uses actual available provenance. Stored index
bands are descriptive, not calibrated probabilities or certificates of identity.
Every integer index0–100 belongs to one stored band, including0–59. Missing or invalid values have a separate category. Old scores/signals may reflect older rules; they are preserved rather than
silently recomputed.

Five geographic cohorts partition analyzed rows: matching GPS name/country;
matching-country GPS without confirmed territorial agreement; matching country
without confirmed GPS; different country; unknown/non-comparable country. All
percentages use analyzed rows. The stored overall counter is shown separately
with an explicit discrepancy notice when it differs.

Reads use exact counts, stable ordering and bounded pagination, failing with503
on missing counts, duplicate IDs, detectable count changes, incomplete reads,
timeout or capacity limits. Current bounds are20,000rows,80requests,6seconds. This
is not a new transactional snapshot: concurrent changes invisible to the checks
remain a limitation. Larger-scale aggregation requires separate work.

API, screen and PDF share the deterministic evidential scope. The widget's existing
aggregate display does not prove per-record methods or independent identity.
Integrity hashes/snapshots and nullifiers are not rewritten by presentation changes.
Public verifiability depends on the actual integrity version and available record;
this methodology is not an external anchor or a universal recovery guarantee.

## Validation boundaries

Tests use synthetic records and provider doubles; browser tests intercept requests.
No real OTP, provider delivery, civil identity, official-domain ownership,
current-role verification or comprehensive historical audit is certified by them.
The candidate introduces no identity-document/KYC collection, new admission
restriction, SQL migration, retention change or automatic task modification.
